About me

How I got here

I’m Cameron Wentink, a mechanical engineering technology graduate based in New Jersey. I like designing and making things, especially when the work calls for careful measurement and a practical understanding of how a part will be used.

Background

Learning, making, improving

I earned an associate degree in Mechanical Engineering Technology. I have experience designing small, intricate custom parts for clients and working as a manual and CNC machinist.

My interests include precision measurement, custom part design, and the process of turning a design into a manufacturable part.

Career direction

I want to keep learning how to design and build useful things while considering the conditions they’ll face in the real world. I’m particularly interested in responsible small-arms design and evaluation.
